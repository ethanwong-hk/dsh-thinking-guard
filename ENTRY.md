- [lemssh77/dsh-thinking-guard](https://github.com/lemssh77/dsh-thinking-guard) - Circuit breaker for pure-thinking idle loops: watches `agent/assistant-stream` and fuses a degenerate turn on three independent gates — no text/tool-call for 45s, reasoning over 80000 chars, or a degenerate repetition pattern (exact repeat unit, sentence-template repeat, sparse repeat, or N-gram density). On trip it cancels the turn and injects a "continue" instruction that forces the next action to be a tool call, so the task resumes instead of stalling. Includes a regression suite (11 cases) and per-gate tuning.
